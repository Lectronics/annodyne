# Attribution

Tracks 2, 4, 13, 28 and 37 of **vocadito** (audio, f0 and note annotations from two annotators),
unmodified copies. Zenodo record 5578259, https://zenodo.org/records/5578259, licence **CC BY 4.0**
(https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/); the supplier's note is in `LICENSE.txt`.

R. M. Bittner, K. Pasalo, J. J. Bosch, G. Meseguer-Brocal, D. Rubinstein, "vocadito: A dataset of solo
vocals with f0, note, and lyric annotations", arXiv:2110.05580, 2021 (citation from memory: verify
against the Zenodo record).

Changes by this project: none to the files; only the subset was selected (singer, pitch range,
language and annotator agreement vary; see `tests/golden/vocals/README.md`).
