# ADR-0022: analyse() finds spectral onsets with the staged pipeline

- **Date:** 2026-10-22
- **Status:** accepted 2026-10-22 (owner: "I do want to go with the new pipeline as the default"); proposed the same day on the owner's request "Lets use analyse() with the new pipeline"; structure review round 1 answered
- **Phase:** 5
- **Amends:** ADR-0010a (the onset strength scale and the energy-step finding, below)

## Context
Phase 3's `detect_onsets` finds spectral onsets with a SuperFlux activation, an adaptive local-mean threshold and an energy refinement, then adds legato energy steps and pitch changes. Phase 5 split the spectral part into public, individually tested stages (`docs/design/phase5-transient-pipeline.md`, `core/analysis/onsets/`) and added what the owner's onset research recommends: level normalisation, a windowed peak-picking rule and a bench of synthetic signals with known onsets. `OnsetParams::method` selects between the Phase 3 chain (`adaptive_threshold`) and the pipeline (`windowed_peaks`); the energy steps, pitch changes, merge and labels are shared.

**Evidence.** All numbers below are reproducible with `build/eval-release/bin/vocadito_report tests/golden/vocals/zenodo_data <set>` (release build; `--tune` = the 35 tuning tracks, `--holdout` = the 5 held-out tracks). Phase 3 rows: `ANNO_TUNE=method=0`; pipeline rows: no override (the analyse() default). The Phase 3 rows equal the figures recorded in ADR-0010a and `docs/design/phase4-pitch-edit.md` (tuning F20 A1 0.582, note pitch 0.853, APC 0.655; held-out F20 A1 0.552, APC 0.552).

| Set | Method | notes | onset F20 A1 / A2 | F50 A1 / A2 | note pitch | APC target | over / under-seg |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 35 tuning | Phase 3 (`method=0`) | 1671 | 0.582 / 0.587 | 0.770 / 0.796 | 0.853 | 0.655 | 0.092 / 0.025 |
| 35 tuning | pipeline | 1696 | 0.612 / 0.622 | 0.772 / 0.800 | 0.857 | 0.659 | 0.096 / 0.025 |
| 5 held-out | Phase 3 (`method=0`) | 280 | 0.552 / 0.528 | 0.727 / 0.774 | 0.803 | 0.552 | 0.115 / 0.043 |
| 5 held-out | pipeline | 274 | 0.585 / 0.569 | 0.731 / 0.771 | 0.811 | 0.567 | 0.108 / 0.047 |

The pipeline gains about +0.03 onset F20 over the Phase 3 chain on both sets (tuning +0.030 / +0.035, held-out +0.033 / +0.041), with note pitch +0.004 / +0.008 and APC +0.004 / +0.015; F50 is unchanged within 0.005 and over/under-segmentation moves by under 0.01 on the tuning tracks (held-out under-segmentation 0.043 -> 0.047). It is one corpus (annotators agree on 75 % of onsets) and the held-out set is 5 tracks (273 notes), so this is a modest gain, not a proven one.

**What made the difference.** With the first version of the pipeline the real-data gain was within noise (F20 0.588 / 0.596, held-out 0.570 / 0.549) and the bench showed legato (family C: 3 of 8) and fast notes (G: 11 of 16, about 23 ms early) as weak. Scoring the same detections at their raw frame times showed the detection was right (G 16 of 16, C 8 of 8) and the *localiser* was losing them: it scanned for the energy's halfway crossing from the start of its +-25 ms window, and in a dense passage the first sample above the halfway level is the previous note's release tail. Starting the scan at the quietest point before the peak (`LocaliseScan::from_quietest_point`, used by the pipeline; the Phase 3 chain keeps `from_window_start` and stays bit-identical) fixed it: bench G 16/0/0, C 7/1/1, and the real-data F20 rose by the 0.03 above. `onset_stages_test` reproduces the mechanism on a two-note signal.

On the research's synthetic families (the only data with every onset known; `tests/support/onset_bench.h`; pooled A, A0, B, D, F, G, +-25 ms; parameters chosen on these signals so optimistic), spectral onsets only (energy steps off): Phase 3 F 0.804 (TP 39 FP 9 FN 10), pipeline F 0.990 (48 / 0 / 1). Full `detect_onsets` as `analyse()` runs it (energy steps on): Phase 3 F 0.545 (39 / 55 / 10), pipeline F 0.750 (48 / 31 / 1). The pipeline finds one soft attack fewer than Phase 3 (family B: 7 of 8 against 8). **The 23 false positives in the single vibrato/tremolo note (D) and the 8 in the noisy family (F) come from the energy-step detector in both methods, not from the spectral chain** (with the steps off both methods have none); on real recordings the steps help (turning them off at an earlier parameter setting: tuning F20 0.597 -> 0.547), so they stay and the synthetic false positives are an open item. Earlier drafts of this ADR and of the design note attributed those false positives to the Phase 3 spectral chain; that was wrong.

Parameter choice (tuning tracks only): a sweep of log gain {1, 10, 100, 1000} x `delta_activation` {0.02, 0.05, 0.1}, then lag, picker windows, level normalisation and `merge_onset_strength` {1, 1.5, 2, 3}. The plateau is F20 0.595-0.60 for gain 1 and delta 0.05-0.1; **0.07 was rejected because it misses all 8 soft attacks of bench family B** (0 of 8; the real-data difference between 0.05 and 0.07 was F20 0.009 on the tuning tracks and 0.016 on the held-out tracks). After the localiser fix the sweep was repeated on the tuning tracks and is flat (F20 0.604-0.615 across gain {1, 10} x delta {0.03-0.1}, lag 10-20 ms, level normalisation on/off, `merge_onset_strength` 2); the defaults stay. `merge_onset_strength` is a trade-off, not a no-op (at 3.0: notes 1661 -> 1551, over-segmentation 0.087 -> 0.057, under-segmentation 0.026 -> 0.038, F20 A2 -0.01); it stays 1.5 (ADR-0010a).

## Decision
1. **`analyse()` uses `SpectralMethod::windowed_peaks`.** `AnalyseParams::onsets` defaults to `analysis_onset_params()`: level normalisation (P0, RMS of the active frames to -23 dBFS), `log_gain` 1, lag 15 ms, windowed peak picking with `delta_activation` 0.05, the research's pre/post windows (30/30/100/70 ms), `min_gap` 30 ms, then localisation that scans from the quietest point before each peak (`LocaliseScan::from_quietest_point`). Energy steps, pitch changes, the merge and the voicing labels are unchanged.
2. **`OnsetParams{}` stays the Phase 3 chain** (`adaptive_threshold`) and is what `detect_onsets` gets when no parameters are named; `params.onsets = OnsetParams{}` (or `ANNO_TUNE=method=0` in the report tool) selects it for `analyse()`. **That is the rollback**: one line, no code removed.
3. **Strength.** Both methods report the activation over its threshold (>= 1 for a picked peak). The scales are not the same quantity (adaptive local mean + spread against local mean + `delta_activation`), so ADR-0010a's `merge_onset_strength` 1.5 and the energy-step strength constants were tuned for the Phase 3 scale. The sweep above kept 1.5; this ADR amends ADR-0010a only to record that the value was re-checked on the pipeline, not re-derived.
4. **Versioning.** There is no params fingerprint on `AnalysisResult` today (`AnalyseParams::version` is the caller's counter), so a stored analysis made with the Phase 3 chain is **not** detected as stale. This is a known gap, not solved here: callers that persist analyses (Phase 7 `project`) must bump `AnalyseParams::version` when onset parameters change, and Phase 7 should add the parameter fingerprint of ADR-0010. Re-analysing changes note boundaries slightly (1671 -> 1682 notes on the tuning set), so stored edits re-map through `remap_after_reanalysis` (src/core/edits/remap.h; tested in `remap_test` and `remap_contract_test` on synthetic note changes; **not exercised on these tracks**).
5. **Golden guards.** `tests/tolerances.h` is the owner's. The measured held-out values sit above its floors, by a wide margin for the onsets and a narrow one for APC (onset F20 floor 0.43 against 0.585/0.569; APC floor 0.55 against 0.567, a margin of 0.017; note pitch floor 0.78 against 0.811), so the guards no longer catch a regression of this size. Raising them is the owner's call and is not done here; the file's comment still quotes F20 0.519 for the held-out set (stale since the c4dm tracker: 0.552 at Phase 3).
6. **Revert criteria.** Revert to the Phase 3 chain if (a) the owner hears worse segmentation on real recordings in the next stage, (b) a second corpus shows held-out onset F20 or APC below the Phase 3 values, or (c) the legato/fast-note work shows the Phase 3 chain is a better base. Each is a parameter change (decision 2).
7. **Known limits.** The energy-step detector false-fires on synthetic tremolo and noise (bench D, F); the legato bench family C is found at the start of the 20 ms cross-fade (about 14 ms before the truth, inside the 25 ms window but not the 20 ms one); one soft attack of family B is missed. Whitening stays off (implemented, form unchecked against Bock 2012).

## Alternatives considered
- Keep the Phase 3 chain: no change; but the staged code is the base for the open work and is not worse on the data we have.
- A larger real-data win by tuning further: rejected, the plateau is flat and the tuning set is also what the sweep was scored on.
- Retune the Phase 3 chain's constants instead (gain 1, other delta): not run; the windowed picker is the research's rule and testable on its own.
- Remove the energy-step detector: rejected, it helps on real recordings (see Evidence).

## Consequences
- `analyse()` output changes (see decision 4); `anno-cli analyse` and the golden numbers move accordingly.
- Both methods stay in the code and under test (`onsets_test` and the bit-exact gate for Phase 3; `onset_stages_test` and the bench for the pipeline), so the choice is a parameter, not a fork.
- The engine tests (`tests/support/engine_fixture.h`) are pinned to the Phase 3 chain: the pipeline places their synthetic note's onset 0.2 ms later, which moves the note's frame grid by one frame and changes a vibrato-extent measure by about 1 % (recorded as an open item in `docs/design/phase4-pitch-edit.md`).
- The legato/fast-note gap is closed on the bench by the localiser fix; real-data testing and the owner's listening are the next checks.
